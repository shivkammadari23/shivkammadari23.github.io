IMPACT-Net: Human-in-the-Loop Explainable AI Engine

Problem Statement

Medical diagnostic AI models often operate as "black boxes." When models predict outcomes without visual backing, domain experts (clinicians) cannot verify the reasoning, leading to low trust in critical environments.

Product Solution & Architecture

  • PRD & Workflow Definition: Defined specifications for a transfer-learning framework (IMPACT-Net) to process NIH chest X-ray datasets.
  • Explainability Integration: Implemented Grad-CAM heatmaps overlaying visual evidence directly onto imaging outputs, ensuring human clinicians retain final decision authority.
  • Domain Shift Mitigation: Structured data preprocessing pipelines to standardize multi-source inputs and eliminate bias.
